01 / ABOUT PALETTE

Designing with intention.
Creating with vision.

Palette Designs offers comprehensive interior design, architectural, and renovation services, from initial consultation to final installation. Our services include design consultation, 2D & 3D visualization, lighting design, project management, and furniture and decor selection.

Every project is an opportunity to innovate and create spaces that reflect YOU. We design functional, aesthetically pleasing, and personalized environments tailored to individual needs, preferences, and budgets.

Inspired by the culture and elegance of neo-modernism, our approach combines thoughtful design, creativity, and practicality. We guide our clients through renovation and execution, ensuring projects stay aligned with their vision, budget, and timeline.

Our open-minded team believes great design comes from people, culture, collaboration, and shared ideas. Beyond aesthetics, we prioritize the health, safety, comfort, and well-being of everyone who experiences the spaces we create.

With projects across residential, commercial, and hospitality spaces throughout India, Palette Designs delivers interior and architectural solutions with a commitment to quality execution and each client's unique requirements.
